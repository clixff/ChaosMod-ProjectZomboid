description=[b]Twitch and YouTube integration[/b] is optional and allows your viewers to vote for the next effect. You can play without it.

description=[b]Q: Mod does not work. What should I do?[/b]
description=A: Make sure you are using the unstable branch of Project Zomboid on Steam.
description=
description=
description=[b]Q: How to disable some effect or change effect settings?[/b]
description=A: You can edit settings of every effect in the mod settings windows (bottom-left corner)
description=
description=[b]Q: I only want to display Twitch or YouTube nicknames above zombies, without random effects and without voting. How can I do this?[/b]

description=[*]Streamer mode enabled: [b]true[/b]
description=[*]Voting enabled: [b]false[/b]
description=[*]Donations enabled: [b]false[/b]
description=[*]Use zombie nicknames: [b]true[/b]
description=[/list]
